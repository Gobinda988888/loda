Three calm checks

Safety is a conversation, not a hidden setting. Keep the child informed and involved.

01 / SOURCE

Know who published it

Use an official source, confirm the publisher name, and scan the file before opening it.

02 / PERMISSIONS

Ask what it needs

Reject access that is unrelated to the app's purpose. Never approve camera, microphone, contacts, or location access by default.

03 / AGREEMENT

Make it visible

Explain what data is collected, where it goes, and how the child can pause or uninstall the app.
